Logo proposal · Theori Dental Clinic, Paphos

A tooth, a smile and a Θ

A calm, modern mark for a family dental practice in Paphos. It is friendly without being childish, and it matches the colours and type of the new website.

The idea

Three ideas in one line drawing

The outline is a molar. Inside it, where the Greek letter Θ (theta) has its crossbar, sits a gentle smile. Theta is the first letter of Θεωρί, so the mark carries the clinic's initial, its Cypriot roots and its promise: "Smile, you're in good hands."

Usage

A few simple rules

Give it room

Keep clear space around the logo at least as wide as the smile inside the mark.

✕

Don't stretch or recolour

Use only the supplied versions, scaled proportionally, in the palette colours.

Minimum sizes

  • Full logo: 120 px or 30 mm wide
  • Mark alone: 16 px or 6 mm
  • Under that, use the mark only

Files

Where the files are

All versions are vector SVGs with the lettering converted to shapes, so they print sharp at any size and need no fonts installed. They are in the brand folder of the website's GitHub repository.
