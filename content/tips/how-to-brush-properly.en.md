Brushing is the single best thing you can do for your smile every day, and the good news is it only takes **two minutes**. The trick is doing those two minutes well.

## What you need

- A **soft** or medium toothbrush with a small head (manual or electric both work great)
- A **fluoride toothpaste**: adults and teens usually use one with 1,350–1,500 ppm fluoride
- A timer, a song you love, or a brush with a built-in timer

## The 2-minute routine

1. **Split your mouth into four parts**: top right, top left, bottom right, bottom left. Give each one about **30 seconds**.
2. **Angle the brush at 45°** towards the gumline. Plaque loves to hide where tooth meets gum.
3. **Use small, gentle circles.** No need to scrub hard: pressing too firmly can wear enamel and irritate gums.
4. **Clean every surface**: the outside, the inside (tilt the brush upright for the back of your front teeth) and the chewing surfaces.
5. **Finish with your tongue** to freshen your breath.
6. **Spit, don't rinse.** Leaving a little toothpaste behind lets the fluoride keep working.

## Don't forget in-between

A toothbrush can't reach between teeth. Once a day, use **interdental brushes or floss** to clean those gaps. Your dentist or hygienist can show you which size suits you.

## Little habits that help

- Brush **last thing at night** and on **one other occasion** during the day.
- After acidic food or drinks (citrus, fizzy drinks, wine), **wait about an hour** before brushing.
- **Replace your brush** (or brush head) every three months, or sooner if the bristles splay.

> **Tip:** If your gums bleed a little when you start cleaning between your teeth, don't stop: it usually settles within a week or two as gums get healthier. If it doesn't, come and see us.
